icon.png is the author's own GitHub avatar (github.com/Jerry-Ma) -- a WoW
screenshot of their character against a desert sunset -- downscaled from the
460x460 original to 128x128.

128x128 because WoW's texture loader wants power-of-two dimensions, and 128
leaves headroom above the ~32-48px the addon list actually draws it at.

Noted here only so a later reader knows where it came from and that it is the
author's own image, not borrowed art with a license to honour (unlike the
NDui_Plus textures under RoleIcons\, which carry their own LICENSE.txt).
